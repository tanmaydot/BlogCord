BlogCord turns your blog into a Discord server: tags are channels, posts are messages, and `Ctrl+K` jumps anywhere.

## What's new

- **Content Collections**: drop a Markdown file in `src/content/posts/` and it appears.
- **Three themes**: dark, light and AMOLED, remembered between visits.
- **Quick switcher**: press `Ctrl+K` (or `Cmd+K`) to search posts.
- **RSS and sitemap** out of the box.

Edit `src/site.config.ts` to make it yours.
